# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: July 28, 2026**

Boardbarian is a board-game rules assistant. This Privacy Policy explains what information Boardbarian collects, how that information is used and retained, when it is shared with service providers, and the choices available to users.

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be sent to **[support@boardbarian.com](mailto:support@boardbarian.com)**.

## 1. Who May Use Boardbarian

Boardbarian is intended only for users who are at least 13 years old.

Boardbarian is not directed to children under 13, and Boardbarian does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child under 13 has provided personal information to Boardbarian, contact **[support@boardbarian.com](mailto:support@boardbarian.com)** so that the information can be reviewed and deleted where appropriate.

## 2. Information Boardbarian Collects

Boardbarian may collect the following categories of information.

### Account information

Boardbarian uses Firebase Authentication to create and manage user accounts. Depending on the authentication method used, account information may include:

* Your name
* Your email address
* Your profile image
* Your Firebase account identifier
* Authentication-provider information
* Information used to maintain and secure your login session

Boardbarian does not receive or store your password when authentication is handled by an external identity provider.

### Conversations and submitted content

Boardbarian stores information you submit while using the service, including:

* Questions and messages
* Conversation history
* Board-game names and related context
* Images and documents you upload
* Feedback you provide
* Generated answers and other model output
* Information used by conversational-memory features

Uploaded images and documents are not intentionally shared with other Boardbarian users.

### Usage, analytics, and technical information

Boardbarian and its service providers may collect technical and usage information, including:

* Pages and features used
* Dates and times of activity
* Browser and device information
* Referring pages
* Approximate geographic information
* IP addresses
* Request metadata
* Application errors
* Security and diagnostic information
* Analytics identifiers and cookies

Boardbarian does not ordinarily store IP addresses in its application database. IP addresses and related request information may appear in Google Cloud, Firebase, Google Analytics, or other infrastructure and security logs.

### Communications

If you contact Boardbarian by email, Boardbarian may retain:

* Your email address
* The contents of your message
* Attachments
* Related replies and support history

Boardbarian uses Gmail for support, account-related, operational, and other email communications.

## 4. Retention and Use for Testing and Improvement

Retention and use of submitted content for testing, evaluation, and improvement is a condition of using Boardbarian.

Boardbarian may retain and use:

* Questions and conversations
* Uploaded images and documents
* Generated responses
* Feedback
* Conversation structure and history
* Related interaction and evaluation data

This information may be used to test, evaluate, maintain, and improve Boardbarian. This includes testing whether the service correctly remembers and uses information across multiple conversations or sessions.

### Pseudonymized testing data

Data selected for testing may be assigned a separate test identifier.

Multiple conversations or interactions originating from the same account may remain associated with the same test identifier when necessary to evaluate conversational memory, multi-session behavior, or related features.

The test identifier is separate from direct account identifiers such as the user’s email address and Firebase account identifier. It is not intended to be used to contact the user or operate the user’s account.

However, pseudonymized testing data is not necessarily anonymous. Questions, messages, images, and uploaded documents may themselves contain names, faces, addresses, numbers, or other information that identifies a person.

Boardbarian does not guarantee that submitted content retained for testing is completely anonymous.

### Sensitive information

Do not submit or upload sensitive personal information, including:

* Social Security numbers
* Government identification documents
* Financial account or payment-card information
* Passwords, API keys, or authentication credentials
* Medical or health records
* Private information about another person
* Confidential employer or business information
* Legally protected or privileged information

Boardbarian may remove sensitive material from testing data when it is identified, but Boardbarian does not review every submission before it is retained or processed.

## 5. Artificial-Intelligence Providers

Boardbarian uses OpenRouter to route requests to third-party artificial-intelligence model providers.

When you submit a question, conversation, image, document, or other content, that content and relevant conversation context may be transmitted to:

* OpenRouter
* One or more artificial-intelligence model providers selected through OpenRouter

The particular provider used for a request may vary.

Boardbarian does not restrict all requests to providers that offer zero data retention or prohibit training. Different providers may have different practices concerning:

* Prompt and response logging
* Data retention
* Human review
* Abuse monitoring
* Model evaluation
* Model improvement or training
* Data location and international processing

By submitting content to Boardbarian, you direct Boardbarian to transmit that content to the providers needed to generate and deliver the requested response.

Boardbarian does not control every downstream provider’s retention, deletion, logging, or training practices. Information already transmitted to a provider may remain subject to that provider’s terms and privacy practices even after you delete your Boardbarian account.

Do not submit confidential, private, or sensitive information to Boardbarian.

## 8. Account Deletion and Data Retention

You may delete your Boardbarian account through the account-deletion feature or by contacting **[support@boardbarian.com](mailto:support@boardbarian.com)**.

When you delete your account:

* Access to the account is disabled immediately. The account is flagged as deleted, and further requests using it are refused from that point on.
* The flagged account and the conversations, messages, and related records associated with it are then permanently removed.
* Permanent removal happens on a periodic basis rather than at a fixed moment. Boardbarian keeps a flagged account for a retention window of at least 90 days before it becomes eligible for removal, and removal is carried out by an operator rather than automatically. Boardbarian does not guarantee that removal occurs on a particular date.
* Some conversations, messages, uploads, generated responses, and related data may continue to be retained, in some cases under a separate test identifier.
* Multiple retained conversations may remain associated with one another.
* Content contained inside retained messages, images, or documents may still include identifying information that you submitted.
* Security records, logs, analytics data, backups, and operational records may remain for their configured retention periods.
* Information previously transmitted to third-party providers may remain subject to those providers’ retention and deletion policies.

Deleting your account therefore does not necessarily delete all content you submitted to Boardbarian.

Boardbarian may also retain information when reasonably necessary to:

* Detect or prevent abuse
* Maintain security
* Resolve disputes
* Enforce applicable terms
* Comply with legal obligations
* Maintain pseudonymized testing and evaluation data as described in this Policy

Where Boardbarian has removed the direct association between your account and retained testing data, Boardbarian may be unable to locate or identify all retained data that originated from your account.
